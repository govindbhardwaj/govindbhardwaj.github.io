01Who we are

The apps covered by this policy are developed and published by Govind Bhardwaj, an independent software developer based in India (“we”, “us”, “our”). We are responsible for your personal data as the data controller under the EU and UK GDPR and as the Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

For any privacy question or request, email govindbhardwaj@outlook.com. This address is also our privacy point of contact and grievance contact. More details are in section 17.

02Scope

This policy applies to every mobile application published under the developer name “Govind Bhardwaj” on Google Play and the Apple App Store, and to the backend services, websites and support channels connected to them (together, the “Apps”).

Each App's store listing has a Data safety section (Google Play) and App Privacy details (App Store) that list the exact data types that App handles. Those disclosures are consistent with this policy. An App never collects more than this policy describes. Before any App starts a new kind of data practice, we will update this policy first.

This policy does not cover third-party websites, services or content that an App links to or displays. See section 15.

03Information we collect

What an App collects depends on its features. An App that does not offer accounts, for example, collects no account information. The categories below are everything any App may collect.

3.1 Information you give us

  • Account information. If an App lets you create an account: your name or display name, email address, password and, optionally, a profile photo. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are stored only as salted hashes. We never see them in plain text.
  • Third-party sign-in. If you choose Sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple, we receive your name, email address and a unique account identifier from that provider. We never receive your Google or Apple password. With Sign in with Apple you can choose to hide your email, in which case we receive an Apple relay address.
  • Content you create. Things you save or post in an App, such as bookmarks, preferences, saved items, comments or uploaded images. We store them so the feature works and syncs across your devices.
  • Messages to us. When you contact support or send feedback: your email address, what you write, and any attachments you choose to include.
  • Purchases. If an App offers in-app purchases or subscriptions, payment is handled entirely by Google Play or Apple. We never receive your card or bank details. We receive a purchase token or receipt, the product, the date and the status, so we can unlock what you bought.

3.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system and version, App version, language, time zone and screen size.
  • App identifiers: a random App instance or installation ID and, if you enable notifications, a push notification token. These identify an installation of the App, not you personally.
  • Usage data (analytics): which screens are opened, which features are used, session length and similar in-app events. We use this in aggregate to understand what works and what to improve.
  • Diagnostics: crash logs, stack traces and performance data, including the device state when a crash happened.
  • Network information: your IP address, which our servers and providers receive whenever an App connects to the internet. It is used for security and to estimate your country or city. We do not use it to find your precise location.
  • Advertising identifier: only in Apps that show ads, as described in section 7. On iOS, we access it only if you give permission.

An App asks for a permission only when you use a feature that needs it, and explains why first. If you decline, the rest of the App keeps working. You can change any permission later in your device settings. The store listing shows the exact permissions each App uses.

3.4 Information we do not collect

The Apps do not collect your contacts or address book, SMS or call logs, background location, health or fitness data, biometric data (Face ID, Touch ID and fingerprint unlock are handled entirely by your device, and we never receive biometric data), bank or card numbers, or government ID numbers. We do not ask for sensitive information such as racial or ethnic origin, religious or political beliefs, health or sexual orientation.

05How we share information

We do not sell your personal data, and we never share it with data brokers.

We share personal data only in these situations:

  • Service providers. Companies that run infrastructure for us, such as hosting, databases, sign-in, analytics, crash reporting, push notifications and email. They process data only on our instructions and only to provide their service to us. Each is bound by contract or by its published data processing terms to protect your data with safeguards that are the same as, or equal to, those described in this policy. The current list is in section 6.
  • App stores. Google and Apple, to process purchases and subscriptions, provide sign-in and distribute the Apps.
  • Advertising partners, only in Apps that contain ads, as described in section 7.
  • Legal reasons. When we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law, regulation, legal process or an enforceable government request, or is needed to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the public or ourselves.
  • Business transfers. If an App is sold, merged or transferred, user data may pass to the new owner. The new owner must keep honouring this policy, and we will tell you before your data becomes subject to a different policy.
  • With your consent, or when you ask us to share something.

We may also share aggregated or de-identified statistics, for example “an App has 10,000 monthly users”, that cannot reasonably be used to identify you.

07Advertising and tracking

Some Apps may be free and show ads. Their store listings are marked “Contains ads”. Apps without that label show no ads and do not access your advertising identifier.

  • iOS. We ask your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before accessing your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) or tracking you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, you will only see non-personalised ads. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android. Ads may use your device's advertising ID. You can reset or delete it in Settings › Privacy › Ads (the exact path depends on your device). Once it is deleted, you will see only non-personalised ads.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland. Before showing personalised ads, we ask for your consent through Google's certified consent message. You can change your choice from the App's settings.

Analytics and crash data are used only to understand and improve our own Apps. They are not combined with data from other companies to target ads, and they are not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

09Data security

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the data we handle, including:

  • Encryption in transit: all communication between the Apps and our servers uses HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or later).
  • Encryption at rest on our cloud providers' infrastructure.
  • Access to production data limited to what is strictly necessary, protected by multi-factor authentication.
  • Passwords stored only as salted hashes by our authentication provider.
  • Regular updates of dependencies and prompt installation of security patches.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law, including within the time limits set by the GDPR and India's DPDP Act.

10Account and data deletion

You can delete your account and its data at any time. Step-by-step instructions are on the Delete your account page.

  • In the App: open Settings (or your Profile), choose Delete account and confirm.
  • Without the App: email govindbhardwaj@outlook.com from the email address on your account, with the App's name. We will verify that the account is yours before deleting it.

What is deleted: your profile, sign-in details, content you created, preferences and the device tokens linked to your account. This is done within 30 days of your request, and we email you when it is complete. What may be kept: purchase records the law requires us to keep, aggregated statistics that do not identify you, and copies in encrypted backups until they are purged within 90 days.

Uninstalling an App does not delete your account. Deleting your account does not cancel subscriptions billed by Google Play or the App Store, so cancel those in your store account first.

Deleting some data only: to delete specific data without closing your account, email us and say what you want removed. Apps without accounts: your data stays on your device. Uninstall the App or clear its data to remove it. To delete analytics or crash data linked to your installation, email us.

11Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • Correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • Delete your data (see section 10);
  • Port your data to another service in a commonly used, machine-readable format;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, including processing based on legitimate interests;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, for example by turning off notifications, tracking or location in your device settings. This does not affect processing done before you withdrew;
  • Opt out of personalised advertising (see section 7);
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights.

To exercise a right, email govindbhardwaj@outlook.com with the App's name and what you would like us to do. We may need to verify your identity, usually by confirming you control the email address on the account. We respond within 30 days, or sooner if your local law requires it, and it is free of charge. You may use an authorised agent where the law allows. If we decline your request, we will explain why, and you can appeal by replying to our decision. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

13Children's privacy

The Apps are general-audience apps and are not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age set by the law in your country (for example, 16 in some EU countries). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under that age. In India, where an App collects personal data from a user under 18, it does so only with verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian, and we never track, behaviourally monitor or target ads at children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal data, email govindbhardwaj@outlook.com and we will delete it promptly.

If an App is ever designed for children, joins Google Play's Families program or is listed in the App Store's Kids category, it will comply with COPPA, the GDPR's rules for children, the Google Play Families Policy and Apple's App Store Review Guidelines for kids apps. It will not show personalised ads, will use only ad and analytics SDKs certified for children's apps (or none), and will not send children's personal data to third parties. That App's store listing will say so.

14International transfers

We are based in India, and our service providers may store and process data in the United States, the European Union and other countries where they operate. These countries may have different data protection laws from yours. When we transfer personal data internationally, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, adequacy decisions and our providers' data processing terms, so that your data stays protected as described in this policy.
