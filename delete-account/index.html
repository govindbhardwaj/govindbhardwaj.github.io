Delete your account

You can ask for your account and its data to be deleted from any app published by Govind Bhardwaj on Google Play or the Apple App Store. You can do it inside the app, or from this page if you no longer have the app installed.

Completed within 30 days Last updated 25 October 2026

01How to request deletion

In the app

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (or your Profile).
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Deletion starts right away.

By email, without the app

  1. Email us from the address linked to your account.
  2. Include the app's name and your account email or username.
  3. We confirm that the account is yours, then delete it and email you when it is done.
Email a deletion request

If you signed in with Apple using “Hide My Email”, send the request from any address and include the relay address shown in your Apple ID settings, so we can find your account.

04Before you delete

  • Subscriptions are not cancelled automatically. Cancel them first in Google Play or the App Store, or you may keep being charged.
  • Deletion is permanent. Once it is done, we cannot restore your account or content.
  • Uninstalling is not deleting. Removing the app from your phone does not delete your account.

05Delete some data, keep your account

To delete specific data without closing your account, such as uploaded images or your content history, email govindbhardwaj@outlook.com with the app's name and what you want removed. We will complete it within 30 days.

Apps without accounts keep your data on your device. To remove it, uninstall the app or clear its data (Android: Settings › Apps › [app] › Storage › Clear data). To delete analytics or crash data linked to your installation, email us.
